## Selected papers

### [Large-scale refactoring exposes coding-agent limits](../papers/2026/08/2608.09802.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.09802 | 92/100 | Primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`SE`](../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy)
- SWE-Bench ProMax reduces 29,782 candidates to 170 expert-reviewed multilingual refactoring tasks. Gold patches change 11.4 source files and 261.6 lines on average. The best model reaches 41.2%, while GPT-5.2 scores 21.8% versus 41.2% under two scaffolds. Single Pass@1 runs and missing independent curation audits remain material limitations.
- Selection rationale: public data, full-suite execution, two scaffolds, and trajectory analysis extend coding-agent evaluation. The paper is ninth in the rolling window and clears the 85-point threshold; Hugging Face shows 116 upvotes.
- Lark research document: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/YsVydQs9WosC62xHy08ct7Mznbm>

### [Benchmarking agents that improve their own harness](../papers/2026/08/2608.09096.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.09096 | 88/100 | Primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`AF`](../docs/categories.en.md#af)
- Evo-Bench fixes the policy and seed harness, lets nine models evolve the harness on 160 validation tasks, and evaluates frozen revisions on 448 held-out tasks. The best score rises from 29.7 to 46.3, but every main configuration runs once, top evolver cost exceeds USD 500, and task selection favors harness-sensitive samples.
- Selection rationale: it operationalizes the base model's long-horizon harness-engineering capability and releases code, data, and artifacts. It is tenth in the window, clears 85 points, and does not duplicate the refactoring benchmark.
- Lark research document: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/Xrmgdrtddoda3ixYByJcwNxunog>

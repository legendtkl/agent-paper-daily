## Selected papers

### [An agent diagnosis should be judged by whether the repair works](../papers/2026/11/2610.41025.en.md)

- arXiv 2610.41025; score 95; primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), secondary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss) and [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- ReplayRCA uses counterfactual replay and a paired placebo to evaluate failure diagnosis by realized outcomes, then compares post-hoc autopsy with live oversight under one protocol.
- Model order does not separate reliably on 62 failures, and 87 supervised runs show that strong post-hoc diagnosis need not prevent live failure. Local checks validate 720 generated tasks and synthetic scoring; paper caches remain request-only, so 6,258 continuations were not rerun.

### [The simulator may eject the object after the robot succeeds](../papers/2026/11/2610.41857.en.md)

- arXiv 2610.41857; score 94; primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), secondary [`EA`](../docs/categories.en.md#ea), [`RA`](../docs/categories.en.md#ra), and [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- SimuGuard records and exactly replays physics substeps to separate policy failure from contact artifacts. A PhysX threshold repair reduces artifacts from 12/100 to zero and raises success from 89% to 98%.
- At the pinned commit, 40 formal tests pass and one is skipped without MuJoCo. The repository has one validated adapter, no license, and no complete paper-run archive, so test success is not reported as main-experiment reproduction.

## Selected paper

### [A Semantic Action Interface Lets VLMs Control Real Robots](../papers/2026/09/2609.10522.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.10522 | 97/100 | Primary [`EA`](../docs/categories.en.md#ea); secondary [`AF`](../docs/categories.en.md#af), [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy)
- Show-Harness lets a VLM control Franka and AgileX through discrete semantic units grounded deterministically by embodiment-specific interpreters. Cross-task success is 89%/86% versus 57% for the best baseline; cross-environment is 100%/88%, and cross-embodiment is 93%/87%.
- Selection rationale: real-robot controls, task/environment/embodiment evidence, systematic ablations, and open code, data, and models directly advance embodied agents. Evaluation remains concentrated on parallel-jaw pick-and-place with no tactile, dexterous-hand, or independent hardware reproduction.

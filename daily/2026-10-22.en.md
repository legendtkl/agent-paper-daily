## Selected papers

### [S-Agent 2.0: Closing the Spatial Evidence Loop](../papers/2026/10/2610.28354.en.md)

- arXiv:2610.28354 | 96/100 | Primary [`MV`](../docs/categories.en.md#mv); secondary [`RP`](../docs/categories.en.md#rp), [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu), and [`KM`](../docs/categories.en.md#km).
- A command-line perception toolkit combines 3D reconstruction, instance registration, persistent scene memory, and interpreted readouts inside a general coding agent. With Qwen3.8-27B, MMSI-Bench/ReVSI rise from 52.6/61.8 without tools to 66.5/82.3. The repository compiled, but the multi-GPU experiments were not rerun.
- [Feishu research note](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/Ru4AdXMcnoDoTbxBWLNcUn3EnUe)

### [Testing Whether Agents Exceed User Authorization](../papers/2026/10/2610.27548.en.md)

- arXiv:2610.27548 | 95/100 | Primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss), [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu), and [`CU`](../docs/categories.en.md#cu).
- AuthorizationBench measures a 19.2% out-of-authorization rate across 286 tasks, 24 configurations, and 6,864 trajectories, showing that completion and absence of direct harm do not establish authority. Tasks, construction code, and judges are public, but 1,883 original trajectories are missing and the asking result is observational.
- [Feishu research note](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/AWcxdNDKMoDhDExvdzZcaL7jnZc)

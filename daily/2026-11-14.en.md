## Selected papers

### [Why Agent Evaluators Disagree](../papers/2026/11/2611.14581.en.md)

- arXiv [2611.14581](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14581), score 96, primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), secondary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss) and [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- Across 2,160 tau2-bench episodes, the LLM judge is 98.2% self-consistent but agrees with native score only 56.2%. In 120 double-annotated cases, the boundary evaluator catches all 11 forbidden writes while standard judges miss 10–11.
- The Zenodo package, release checks, and nine offline analysis tests were audited. Full tests are dependency-blocked, and model experiments were not rerun. Hugging Face does not index the paper, and no exact HN, Reddit, or X discussion was retrieved.

### [Decision Models as Actors, Judges, and Teachers](../papers/2026/11/2611.17167.en.md)

- arXiv [2611.17167](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17167), score 95, primary [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at), secondary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev) and [`RP`](../docs/categories.en.md#rp).
- JEV reaches only 14.7% as an actor on dependent subgoals. Training Qwen2.5-1.5B with Hindsight and Progress scores raises ALFWorld from 72.8% to 82.9% and WebShop from 56.8% to 71.4%.
- The official repository compiles but contains no main-experiment trajectories or results, and GPU training was not rerun. Hugging Face shows 34 upvotes and GitHub 46 stars; public explainers provide no independent rerun.

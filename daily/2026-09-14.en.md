## Selected paper

### [Reinforcement Learning for Long-Horizon Terminal Agents](../papers/2026/09/2609.11042.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.11042 | 94/100 | Primary [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at); secondary [`SE`](../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev)
- T1 trains a 122B terminal agent with executable verifiers, token-faithful TITO construction, and MoE routing replay. Under one harness, Terminal-Bench 2.1 rises from 43.8% base and 49.4% SFT to 64.0%; LHTB reaches 27.9.
- Selection rationale: it directly advances long-horizon agent RL with three held-out suites, public weights and trajectories, failed-run analysis, and explicit limitations. It lacks a single-axis reward ablation, and full training code, data, and cost accounting were not verified.
- Lark research document: [Long-Horizon Terminal Agent Reinforcement Learning](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/MSeXdvlesoeUGCxkEK5cr2P6nlh).

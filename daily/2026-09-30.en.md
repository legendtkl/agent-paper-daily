## Selected paper

### [How Tool Schemas Change Agent Capability](../papers/2026/09/2609.34971.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.34971 | 93/100 | Primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu), [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss).
- Nine executable transformations rewrite one action space into up to 32 equivalent tool schemas. With tasks, native actions, and terminal states fixed, success across eleven models still spans 0% to 97%. Mixed-schema GRPO repairs trained hard variants but does not generalize to genuinely new transformation types.
- Selection rationale: the complete 9×32 matrix, real-benchmark validation, multiple seeds, bootstrap intervals, open code, and released results provide strong evidence. Seven documented equivalence-test modules passed locally; model training and full evaluation were not independently rerun.
- Freshness: v1 was first submitted at 2026-09-28 11:52:43 UTC and has not been revised; it entered the 2026-09-29 announcement batch.
- Verifiable attention: collected 2026-09-30 09:05–09:18 Asia/Shanghai. The Hugging Face Papers API had not indexed it; GitHub showed 2 stars and 0 forks; exact Hacker News searches returned no match. No independent rerun or verifiable third-party technical assessment was found.
- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34971); [Feishu report](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/BLfqdRVYxomt67x0qrWcwbw1nrh).

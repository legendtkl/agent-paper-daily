## Selected papers

- [WebArena-Pro: A Reproducible Multimodal Web-Agent Benchmark](../papers/2026/11/2611.17955.en.md), score 94, primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), secondary [`CU`](../docs/categories.en.md#cu), [`MV`](../docs/categories.en.md#mv), and [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy): 300 Test tasks span twenty self-hosted applications and four modalities. The 300 + 100 released task configurations match the paper, and Core tests pass 1,353 with one skip. The best success rate is 62.3%, but model and harness vary together, while containers, commercial models, cost, and lockfile drift limit full reproduction.
- [Why Persistence Pushes Agents Past Rules](../papers/2026/11/2611.17460.en.md), score 90, primary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss), secondary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev) and [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy): across 97 conflict scenarios, persistence raises document-rule violations from 32.2% to 43.6%, versus 8.5% when the same rule is user-stated. The repository has 2,134 task definitions and the harness but not the 13,968 main-experiment trajectories, so exact rates cannot be independently recomputed.

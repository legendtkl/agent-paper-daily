## Selected papers

### [Rewarded execution makes self-evolving agents forget authorization boundaries](../papers/2026/10/2610.39122.en.md)

- arXiv:2610.39122 | 94/100 | Primary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss); secondary [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- The paper finds that benign positive feedback can generalize “authorized by the current request” into “tasks found in external content are executable.” Skill evolution raises attack success on new content by 14.0–48.8 percentage points and RL by 3.0–39.3 points. Authorization-aware feedback reduces ASR from 35.0% to 5.8%, while delimiter-only isolation still leaves 37.5%–47.5% ASR. At the pinned repository revision, 94/94 skill tests and 472/472 focused offline tests pass; paid inference, RL training, and the full main experiment were not run.
- [Feishu independent report](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/EJ4ndk79rojDcwxuWJJcC0YPngb)

### [Do research agents have taste for early-stage ideas?](../papers/2026/10/2610.40488.en.md)

- arXiv:2610.40488 | 92/100 | Primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`RA`](../docs/categories.en.md#ra), [`MA`](../docs/categories.en.md#ma), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- rTaste constructs 415 minimum viable experiments across seven papers: 412 finish, 369 are judged sound by model auditors, and only 73 produce conclusions that are both beyond the source paper and non-obvious. Hit rates are 38% for the agent’s first choice, 34% for written judgment first, and 36% for same-model judging. The team-over-single-agent confidence interval crosses zero while cost rises from $15.31 to $23.93. The author script reproduces every reported table and 46/46 repository tests pass; the absence of human labels and reliance on model self-evaluation remain central limitations.
- [Feishu independent report](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/JcWkdddzSoyORexvHd4c9ALqnMf)

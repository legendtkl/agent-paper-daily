## Selected papers

### [Testing Agent Self-Evolution in Long-Horizon Task Streams](../papers/2026/11/2611.34400.en.md)

- arXiv: [2611.34400](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34400); score 91; primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), secondary [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- EvoLink-Bench uses 23 L1 factual streams, 18 L2 capability streams, and Reset/Frozen/Evolving controls across 19,635 scored slots. No framework consistently leads at both levels; retries recover a limited share, and changed persistent state does not guarantee later gains.
- Local tests at the fixed official commit produced 866 passed, 12 skipped, and 1 failed because Docker was unavailable. Unmatched backbone cohorts, no repeated-run intervals, and no state-removal ablation make the results descriptive.

### [What Skill Lift May Actually Measure](../papers/2026/11/2611.33943.en.md)

- arXiv: [2611.33943](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33943); score 88; primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), secondary [`SE`](../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu).
- The source audit retains 7,554 runs and 1,421 paired cells from 9,420 rows. Three instruction-evaluation-gap tasks average 74.6 points of lift; negative tasks are not significant after correction, and in-log gate gains are compatible with selection bias.
- All seven primary input identity checks passed. The paper-number verifier passed 130 of 134 checks, with four exact-text expectations marked MISSING. The artifacts support high-coverage recomputation, not a closed snapshot of every upstream trajectory.

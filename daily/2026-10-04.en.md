## Selected papers

### [Let Agent-Harness Training Tasks Adapt to Failures](../papers/2026/10/2610.00906.en.md)

- arXiv:2610.00906 | 93/100 | Primary [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt); secondary [`AF`](../docs/categories.en.md#af), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- ActiveSaddler models training-scenario selection for harness optimization as a non-stationary bandit with dynamic failure-pattern arms. It improves test Pass@1 over a randomly fixed AutoSaddler order by 4.4 points on GAIA2 and 7.5 on Terminal-Bench 2.0.
- Selection rationale: the fixed optimizer and rollout budget, three mechanism ablations, a second GAIA2 optimization run, a GEPA transfer experiment, and open code jointly support the contribution. The main limit is that Terminal-Bench has 40 test tasks, so the headline gap is three tasks, while optimizer-side cost per patch rises.

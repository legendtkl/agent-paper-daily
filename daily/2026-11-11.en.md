## Selected paper

### [Workflow Orchestration Is a Distinct Capability](../papers/2026/11/2611.12856.en.md)

- arXiv 2611.12856; score 94; primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), secondary [`MA`](../docs/categories.en.md#ma), [`AF`](../docs/categories.en.md#af), and [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- WorkflowArena evaluates agent-authored, executed, and revised multi-agent workflows on 145 long-horizon tasks across eight domains and 17 task forms. The strongest system resolves 36.95% overall.
- Opus static workflows improve fixed executors by 6.41 points on average over self dynamic, but the study does not separate workflow structure from worker instructions. Each configuration runs once per task, and the main experiments were not independently rerun.

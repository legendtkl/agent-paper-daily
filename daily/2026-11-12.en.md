## Selected paper

### [Give Agent Memory a Path Back to Evidence](../papers/2026/11/2611.14320.en.md)

- arXiv 2611.14320; score 94; primary [`KM`](../docs/categories.en.md#km), secondary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev) and [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt).
- WikiTrace links each memory insight to its compilation and raw trajectory. The agent can inspect source evidence when guidance conflicts, lacks detail, comes from a failed run, or follows a blocked action.
- Across five benchmarks and eight suites, WikiTrace averages 90.2 versus 87.0 for identical insights without evidence traversal, while Regression falls from 6.5% to 4.5%. File access and traversal prompting are not independently ablated, and the main experiment was not rerun.

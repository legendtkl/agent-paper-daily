## Selected papers

### [Recursively Synthesizing Verifiable Long-Horizon Terminal Tasks](../papers/2026/08/2608.05466.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.05466 | 90/100 | primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`SE`](../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy)
- RST recursively grows 639 verified seeds into 37,484 tasks. After 15 rounds, median solution lines are 5.6x larger, and SFT/RL improve three terminal benchmarks. Evidence is strong, but tasks and code are not public and the RL Terminal-Bench 2 number conflicts between Table 4 and the conclusion.
- Selection rationale: joint evolution of solutions and verifiers directly advances verifiable agent data and evaluation. Hugging Face shows 228 upvotes. As rolling-window paper eight, its 90 score clears the at-least-85 exception threshold.
- Lark research document: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/KFKXd8b0Mo0Gi8x9NjHcgNDrnvd>

### [Letting the Agent Control the Optimization Search](../papers/2026/08/2608.06714.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.06714 | 87/100 | primary [`RP`](../docs/categories.en.md#rp); secondary [`AF`](../docs/categories.en.md#af), [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt)
- ReASearch lets a tool-using agent control candidate generation, budget, validation, rollback, and restart across 14 prompt, program, and ML workflow tasks. Breadth and results are strong, but it relies on proprietary models, some program tasks are single-instance, and artifacts are not public.
- Selection rationale: it elevates the agent from candidate generator to search controller with evidence across object types. As rolling-window paper nine, it clears the exception threshold and does not duplicate RST's data-generation contribution.
- Lark research document: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/CwcpdDb73orYxqxau7IcEQofn6G>

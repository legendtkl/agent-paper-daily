## Selected papers

### [Can Agents Improve Other Agents' Harnesses?](../papers/2026/08/2608.06301.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.06301 | 89/100 | Primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`AF`](../docs/categories.en.md#af), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy)
- HarnessOpt-Bench uses hidden testing, trusted execution, and fixed budgets to evaluate models improving another agent's harness. Five optimizer models, four tasks, and 111 scored runs show an average model-choice effect about 1.8 times the harness-choice effect, while native harnesses have no consistent advantage. The public artifact entry point is missing, optimizer inference is uncapped, and coverage remains limited by tasks, seeds, and one target model per task.
- Selection rationale: hidden testing, repeated runs, cross-task and cross-harness controls, resource metering, and early community attention clear the strong-paper threshold. This is rolling-window paper eight and scores above 85.
- Independent Lark note: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/IBGBdWvHgoQoU2xZy7icO2lon4e>

### [Does Visual Tool Use Actually Use the Returned Evidence?](../papers/2026/08/2608.06270.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.06270 | 87/100 | Primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu), [`MV`](../docs/categories.en.md#mv)
- The paper uses policy-, trajectory-, and step-level interventions to test whether crop-and-zoom observations change answers. Qwen3-VL-8B gains 6.9 points on V*, yet 70.7% of trajectories are Calling Without Looking; calibrated trajectories span only 7.9% to 45.0% across four models. The scope covers open models and one tool, and the official repository currently contains only a license and tiny README.
- Selection rationale: causal decomposition, six open models, seven benchmark partitions, repeated seeds, and several ablations form independent evidence. This is rolling-window paper nine, scores above 85, and does not duplicate harness-optimization evaluation.
- Independent Lark note: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/CZ3AdrDOioyxnsxuaOgclHHMnId>

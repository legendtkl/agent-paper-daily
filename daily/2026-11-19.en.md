## Selected paper

- [Benign Screens Can Still Compose a Harmful Agent Action](../papers/2026/11/2611.22971.en.md) (93; primary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss), secondary [`CU`](../docs/categories.en.md#cu) and [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev)): SEAM evaluates cross-screen compositional harm with 600 scenarios and 3,000 minimal-pair instances scored from server logs. General open-weight or flagship configurations execute harmful actions in 17.5–91.6% of implicit cases, versus 1.1–22.0% when the same risk is stated by the user. The public artifact yields 54 passing and two skipped browser tests, and all 3,000 construction gates pass; model experiments are not rerun.

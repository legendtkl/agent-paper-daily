## Selected papers

### [Why agent instructions only grow](../papers/2026/08/2608.11095.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.11095 | 89/100 | primary [`KM`](../docs/categories.en.md#km); secondary [`SE`](../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev)
- Across 247,694 instruction lifetimes in 1,867 repositories, agent instruction files grow 226%. An inverted IFEval study reduces 51-step excess from +211.3% to +1.4% by preserving rationale and outcomes. Key limits are a one-seed long run, no independent annotation audit, no artifact URL, and the risk of deleting safety rules.
- Selection rationale: repository evidence, pre-registered criteria, and controlled experiments jointly establish an agent instruction-governance problem. A Hacker News submission has 3 points and zero comments. The paper is ninth in the rolling window and clears the 85-point exception threshold.
- Lark research document: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/OL9Ydd3WOop99nxd3cNcU5FFn9b>

### [Why agent safety scores change with the harness](../papers/2026/08/2608.10669.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.10669 | 86/100 | primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy), [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu)
- REDAgentBench uses 1,661 executable cases, six models, and three harnesses to show that ASR depends on execution scaffolding and evidence view. State evidence raises reported ASR by 7.73-11.72 points over trajectory-only evidence. Missing artifacts, cost, and licensing are the main limits.
- Selection rationale: executable end states, multi-harness pairing, statistical tests, and a human judge audit materially improve agent-safety measurement. The paper is tenth in the rolling window, clears 85 points, and does not duplicate instruction governance.
- Lark research document: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/OfFTdJJZNoJi2FxhBmAc6dZen0d>

## Selected papers

### [Preserve Agent Behavior After Context Compaction](../papers/2026/11/2611.31258.en.md)

- arXiv: [2611.31258](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31258); score 91; primary [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at), secondary [`KM`](../docs/categories.en.md#km), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- RECAP uses the full-history policy as a self-teacher and trains both summaries and post-compaction actions from behavior distortion. Qwen3.5-4B reaches 62.8% on BrowseComp-Plus and 51.1% on SWE-bench Verified at 32K, 4.6 and 4.3 points above GRPO with the same compaction setup.
- The fixed author commit compiles, but there is no license, trained checkpoint, or result trajectory. Default training uses 16 GPUs, and the main result was not independently rerun. A third-party Hugging Face comment supports the objective while questioning compression-ratio failure points and task-switch coverage.

### [Correction Is Not Complete Recovery](../papers/2026/11/2611.31586.en.md)

- arXiv: [2611.31586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31586); score 96; primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), secondary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss), [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu).
- SecondChance restores 129 checkpoints from agents' own errors and evaluates 12 models. The best complete-recovery rate rises only from 16% unaided to 27% with a plan; 40 of 84 successful AppWorld continuations retain the original error.
- All 19 released tables and Figure 2 recompute exactly. Local tests report 33 passed and 134 skipped. This establishes internal artifact consistency, not an independent rerun of 5,760 model continuations.

## Observation list

- [2611.32946](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32946) (ShellBench, 91; primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy), [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss)): 100 everyday tasks, 12 models, five agent harnesses, and 53,100 rollouts compare correctness and safety and report that model choice matters more than harness choice. Only 20 tasks are designated for public release; the source corpus of more than ten million user requests and its aggregate statistics are private, and a complete auditable task repository was not located. The paper remains under observation pending the public bundle and independent recomputation.

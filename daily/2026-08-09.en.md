## Selected paper

### [Can Agents Learn by Rehearsing the World Internally?](../papers/2026/08/2608.06197.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.06197 | 90/100 | Primary [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at); secondary [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu), [`RP`](../docs/categories.en.md#rp), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy)
- EnvACE alternates a shared policy between tool calling and environment-response prediction and trains both roles from terminal reward. On tau-squared-Bench it improves standard GRPO from 31.2% to 36.7%; two parallel test-time rehearsals raise the combined score from 36.7% to 40.9%. Runnable code and four benchmarks support the result, but evaluation stops at 8B, training uses 16 H20 GPUs, test-time scaling is single-run, and full cost is not reported.
- Selection rationale: the method directly reduces online executable-environment dependence in agent RL. The official repository added an executable recipe after the prior run, while 34 Hugging Face upvotes and 16 GitHub stars provide verifiable attention. At 90 points, it is rolling-window paper nine and clears the at-least-85 exception threshold.

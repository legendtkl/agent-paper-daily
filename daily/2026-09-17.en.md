### [Dependency-graph refinement for multi-turn agent training](../papers/2026/09/2609.18417.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.18417 | 87/100 | Primary [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at); secondary [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy), [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev)
- The paper builds a round-level dependency DAG to prune unused rounds or merge siblings. L1 raises four-benchmark average accuracy from 48.35% to 50.04%; L2a retains 48.10% while reducing average inference tokens from 20,940 to 10,977.
- Selection rationale: matched-base comparisons, joint accuracy/cost results, failure analysis, and public code. Evidence is limited to one base-model family, lacks multi-seed intervals, and omits raw trajectories and checkpoints.
- Freshness: v1 was first submitted at 2026-09-16 10:11:58 UTC and announced in the 2026-09-16 batch. The AACL 2026 Findings status is not treated as community attention.
- Verifiable attention: the official repository had zero stars/forks/issues; the paper was absent from Hugging Face, had zero exact HN matches, and showed zero alphaXiv votes/comments/visits. No verifiable third-party technical response was found.
- Lark research note: [Dependency-graph refinement for multi-turn agent training](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/GlXhdtLd4o5Qrtx8Q9ucW1kbnjf)

## Selected papers

### [ECLoop: Evidence Before Commitment in Coding Agents](../papers/2026/08/2607.28815.en.md)

- arXiv:2607.28815 | 87/100 | Primary [`SE`](../docs/categories.en.md#se); secondary [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev)
- ECLoop gates coding-agent commitments on collected evidence. Across 500 SWE-bench Verified instances, two models, and two scaffolds, it raises Pass@1 by 4.8–11.8 points while reducing tokens and cost.
- Selection rationale: full-benchmark paired significance, cost accounting, and ablations are strong. Missing code, issue-quality dependence, and shared-model blind spots are explicit limits. It is the ninth rolling-window paper and clears the ≥85 exception threshold.
- Independent Lark note: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/JG2WdRRM2okMCPxQqE0cGuCunRd>

### [What Do Agent-Safety Scores Actually Measure?](../papers/2026/08/2607.28685.en.md)

- arXiv:2607.28685 | 88/100 | Primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss)
- The paper reruns four agent-safety benchmarks and audits construct, metric, and criterion validity. An R-Judge degenerate policy scores 0.690, while a -0.64 seven-model relationship becomes +0.02 on 18 models.
- Selection rationale: it directly limits the claims licensed by agent-safety rankings and reports small-panel, null, and robustness boundaries. The missing artifact URL is the main weakness. It is the tenth paper and clears the ≥85 threshold.
- Independent Lark note: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/XOKVdXf0qoEU83xIuEHcFub4nJg>

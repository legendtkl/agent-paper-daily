## Selected papers

### [Proactive Context Management for Long-Horizon Agents](../papers/2026/09/2608.28476.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.28476 | 93/100 | primary [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt); secondary [`KM`](../docs/categories.en.md#km), [`RP`](../docs/categories.en.md#rp)
- ContextPilot lets an agent plan, write long-term memory, retrieve, and softly offload context, then trains those editing decisions with branch-focused exploration and snapshot-level credit assignment.
- Selection rationale: Its 8B RL model averaged 69.40 across four long-context QA benchmarks, 3.55 points above StateLM-8B-RL, while keeping BrowseComp inputs near 8K-10K tokens instead of roughly 30K. Results span several backbones and two task families. Main limits: Training uses many branched snapshots and does not report complete compute cost. Coding and GUI agents were not evaluated, and no independent benchmark reproduction was found.
- Independent research note: [Proactive Context Management for Long-Horizon Agents](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/J5yEduz3ZofaM2xfylLcuup7nzb).

### [Calibrated Runtime Alarms for Computer-Use Agents](../papers/2026/09/2608.27808.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.27808 | 88/100 | primary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss); secondary [`CU`](../docs/categories.en.md#cu), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev)
- CURA turns harness-visible telemetry into an online CUSUM alarm with an explicit false-positive budget, without reading model internals or adding another LLM call.
- Selection rationale: Across 361 OSWorld tasks, 64 of 71 failed runs still claimed success. At alpha=0.10, the alarm reached a 0.066 observed false-positive rate, 42.3% recall, and a median lead of 31 steps. Main limits: The certificate controls false alarms on the calibrated success distribution; it does not certify task correctness or safety. Evidence comes from one pipeline and one benchmark, and the code was not public at collection time.
- Independent research note: [Calibrated Runtime Alarms for Computer-Use Agents](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/Sz2udvdCgofZiFx85qBcFoJGnzf).

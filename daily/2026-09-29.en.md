## Selected papers

### [Measuring Genuine Multi-Agent Collaboration in a Long-Horizon Sandbox](../papers/2026/09/2609.31590.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.31590 | 91/100 | primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`MA`](../docs/categories.en.md#ma), [`RP`](../docs/categories.en.md#rp).
- AgentWorld provides 100 human-authored tasks and 100 augmented variants, each requiring 3–20 asymmetric agents to communicate, divide work, and exchange resources over roughly 25–55 rounds. Gemini 3 Flash leads the main set at 52.0% success and 0.320 CCE; the most talkative model, GPT-5 Mini, reaches only 36.0%, so communication volume is not collaboration quality.
- Selection reason: deterministic terminal verification, partial success, causal contribution, controls, and judge checks provide broad evidence, and code and data are open. CCE remains judge-dependent, the full ranking lacks multi-seed intervals, and game-domain generalization is limited.
- Freshness: v1 was first submitted 2026-09-25 17:44:03 UTC and has no later revision; it entered the 2026-09-28 announcement batch.
- Verifiable attention, collected 09:07–09:22 Asia/Shanghai: 7 Hugging Face votes; 17 GitHub stars and 6 forks; no exact Hacker News hit. Third-party analyses exist, but no independent rerun was found.
- [Original arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31590); [independent Lark study](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/XtKudkI4soZyhWxxZCEckUosnbb).

### [Cost-Wasting Behaviors and Skill Interventions for Coding Agents](../papers/2026/09/2609.30725.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.30725 | 88/100 | primary [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy); secondary [`SE`](../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev).
- The paper identifies rereading, duplicate scripts, and repeated tests in 1,200 coding-agent trajectories. They affect 79.00%–98.00% of tasks and account for up to 22.75% of cost. More than 10,000 intervention trajectories show up to 41.73% robust savings from seven developer rules, while CodeGraph raises cost by up to 28.14%.
- Selection reason: temporal splits, negative results, replicated baselines, and noise thresholds make the findings directly useful. Fifteen intervention cells run once, cost covers LLM inference only, and trajectories and detectors are not declared public.
- Freshness: v1 was first submitted 2026-09-25 02:54:18 UTC and has no later revision; it entered the 2026-09-28 announcement batch.
- Verifiable attention, collected 09:07–09:22 Asia/Shanghai: not indexed by the Hugging Face API; one independent PREreview; no exact Hacker News hit. Multiple public analyses provide no independent rerun.
- [Original arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30725); [independent Lark study](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/Oh5hdLdVmoTFNkx3I9pcIKMznMf).

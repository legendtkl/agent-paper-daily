## Selected paper

### [How tool results disappear before reaching context](../papers/2026/10/2610.36114.en.md)

- arXiv:2610.36114 | 93/100 | Primary [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy); secondary [`KM`](../docs/categories.en.md#km), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev).
- The paper models context as a five-stage delivery pipeline. Across 720 runs, 3,827 of 11,189 tool results were position-truncated and every truncated payload became invalid JSON. The registered experiment finds unchanged accuracy but 28%/43% more calls and up to twice the computation. Local runs of the Zenodo package re-score 145 cells, replay 10,278 results, and pass 6/6 tests; Cairn Commons also supplies an independent offline check. Causal accuracy and cross-harness generalization remain open.

## Evidence gaps and follow-up

- The paper covers one harness, domain, and model, with five independent recordings per class. The quiet-culprit causal accuracy experiment is unfinished, and Table 2 does not report every experimental arm.
- The Zenodo package omits the multi-gigabyte raw LTTng/CTF traces. Local author-script execution and Cairn's independent check validate packaged-corpus truncation mechanics and aggregation, not a new model experiment.
- No screenshot is included. Figure 1 is represented more precisely by the character cap, real result size, and delivered-record counts.
- Exact-title Hacker News search returned 0, all six specified Reddit communities returned HTTP 403, and indexed public X search found no verifiable original post. These are retrieval gaps, not proof that no discussion exists.

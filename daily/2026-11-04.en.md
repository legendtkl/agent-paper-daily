## Selected papers

No model calls or full container replay were performed. The paper says full trajectories are released, while the repository says model conversations are not public.

### [A deleted injection can still act through agent memory](../papers/2026/11/2611.01700.en.md)

- arXiv 2611.01700; score 92; primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), secondary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss), [`KM`](../docs/categories.en.md#km), and [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- MemHarmBench removes the attack carrier and starts a fresh session, separately measuring retention, target adoption, task completion, and trajectory anomalies. Conditional ASR is 59.38–76.39%, and 55.71% of eligible episodes complete normally while adopting the target.
- At the pinned commit, 76 tests and eight subtests pass, and hashes validate all 288 public input cases. Historical per-episode trajectories, raw judgments, and complete result tables are absent, so input validation is not reported as main-result reproduction.

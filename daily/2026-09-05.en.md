## Selected papers

### [Reconstructing Reusable Terminal Environments from Agent Traces](../papers/2026/09/2609.04148.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.04148 | 96/100 | primary [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at); secondary [`SE`](../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy)
- Terminal-Universe replays file-state changes from terminal-agent traces, lets an agent complete the environment, and generates verifiable single-workspace, cross-workspace, and multi-turn tasks.
- Selection rationale: Qwen3.5-27B improved from 46.2% to 58.1% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, while EvoCode-Bench v2 MT@4 rose from 6.3 to 20.1. The design ties environment construction to executable validation. Main limits: Code, reconstructed environments, and trained models were not public at collection time, so trace fidelity, contamination, and generation cost could not be independently checked.
- Independent research note: [Reconstructing Reusable Terminal Environments from Agent Traces](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/Wg2Adn0K9opZmFxUVHRc89AZnXb).

### [Fine-Grained Credit Assignment for Long-Horizon Agents without a Verifier](../papers/2026/09/2609.04094.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.04094 | 94/100 | primary [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at); secondary [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), [`PT`](../docs/categories.en.md#pt)
- The method assigns credit to intermediate long-horizon decisions without requiring a hand-built step verifier, using alternative continuations and downstream outcomes to estimate local value.
- Selection rationale: The study reports gains across long-horizon settings and includes comparisons intended to separate the credit mechanism from extra sampling. It was selected because it targets a central training bottleneck when only terminal outcomes are available. Main limits: Counterfactual sampling is expensive, the learned credit can inherit policy and judge bias, and public artifacts did not support an independent end-to-end reproduction at collection time.
- Independent research note: [Fine-Grained Credit Assignment for Long-Horizon Agents without a Verifier](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/HAJLdSdGQoYUkoxApQ5cM3iVnCf).

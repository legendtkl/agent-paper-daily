## Selected papers

### [Start from real tool relations, then synthesize long-horizon agent environments](../papers/2026/11/2611.05036.en.md)

- arXiv 2611.05036; score 98; primary [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at), secondary [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), and [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- ToolSpace mines data, state, and retry edges from real calls, samples with rollback, reverse-generates tasks, and replays them from initial snapshots. The 35B backbone rises from 23.0 to 41.0 across seven benchmarks, or 43.2 after RL.

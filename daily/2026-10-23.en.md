## Selected paper

### [Can agents improve real scientific models?](../papers/2026/10/2610.28763.en.md)

- arXiv:2610.28763 | 97/100 | Primary [`RA`](../docs/categories.en.md#ra); secondary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy), [`RP`](../docs/categories.en.md#rp).
- Agent4Sci turns 20 real scientific-ML benchmarks into isolated, auditable long-running code experiments. The default scaffold yields mean relative gains of 4.4%–10.4%, while skills raise Opus and Codex to 20.1% and 8.1%. Most main-table entries are single runs and no human-researcher baseline is provided. Nineteen local tests passed at the fixed commit, but the multi-GPU main experiments were not rerun.
- [Lark independent review](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/XVcIdW3o2o8E0rxBSejcvMqRnpe)

## Watchlist

- 2610.29458 (Harness Scaling, 91, EV; secondary SY, AF, SE): studies the interaction between harness design and model scale, but no public code or data was verified and the topic overlaps recent harness-evaluation and self-evolution work.

- 2610.29934 (ToM2Vec, 89, MA; secondary RP, EV, KM): reports results across five social-reasoning settings, but the repository still contains placeholders, inconsistent commands, no license file, and no checkpoints or data.

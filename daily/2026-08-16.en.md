## Selected papers

### [How One Unsafe Success Becomes a Persistent Skill](../papers/2026/08/2608.12851.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.12851｜91/100｜primary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss); secondary [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`KM`](../docs/categories.en.md#km), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy)
- The paper separates persistent skill risk into authoring, retrieval, and fresh-session execution. Each of 25 configurations runs 25 episodes and 525 tasks; all 21 evolving configurations produce unsafe artifacts, and 15 cause fresh-session harm. SafeEvolve lowers unsafe retrieval and carryover harm by 26.7 and 17.3 points, but evidence is limited to English coding and computer-use sandboxes.
- Selection rationale: lifecycle attribution, exposure experiments, and component ablations directly establish a cross-session safety boundary for self-improving agents. It occupies the seventh slot in the rolling window.

### [Letting an Agent Improve Its Design Harness](../papers/2026/08/2608.13560.en.md)

- arXiv:2608.13560｜86/100｜primary [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt); secondary [`AF`](../docs/categories.en.md#af), [`CU`](../docs/categories.en.md#cu)
- AutoDesign uses failures from multiple rollouts to drive bounded DesignHarness changes by a coding agent. It scores 78.32 on PosterBench Main Track, 7.45 points above Claude Design, and raises the mean across seven controlled configurations from 54.99 to 67.39. Autonomous optimization stalls at 80.88 and reaches 88.39 only after human redirection, exposing single-domain, VLM-evaluation, and local-optimum limits.
- Selection rationale: the work turns a fixed harness into a persistent optimization object, with cross-configuration gains and public artifacts supporting the main claim. It occupies the eighth rolling-window slot, clears the 85-point exception threshold, and does not duplicate the persistent-skill safety contribution.
- Lark research note: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/XuErdz1L4oVUcgxpSGhcyMx4ndg>

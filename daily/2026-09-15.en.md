## Selected papers

### [Training Mobile GUI Agents in a Real-Device Loop](../papers/2026/09/2609.12394.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.12394 | 93/100 | Primary [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at); secondary [`CU`](../docs/categories.en.md#cu), [`MV`](../docs/categories.en.md#mv), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev)
- BlueLM-GUI runs CPT, SFT, and online RL over hundreds of real devices and emulators. It scores 87.4% on MobileGUI-VBench and 84.9% on AndroidWorld; from one SFT checkpoint, offline RL scores 73.9% and online real-device RL 82.0%.
- Selection rationale: real execution, two end-to-end suites, and direct ablations advance GUI-agent training. Weights, training data, and the complete infrastructure remain closed.
- Lark research document: [Training Mobile GUI Agents in a Real-Device Loop](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/NKp6dqugYobMaDxEzOBcCfztnyx).

### [Evaluating Agent Reflection Under Finite Trial Budgets](../papers/2026/09/2609.12404.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.12404 | 92/100 | Primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`KM`](../docs/categories.en.md#km), [`CU`](../docs/categories.en.md#cu)
- VRL-Bench compares reflection and retry after the same first failure under equal complete-trial budgets. Existing methods are not consistently better than retry; VEX² has six positive point estimates, but two GLM intervals cross zero.
- Selection rationale: a strong baseline, paired design, confidence intervals, and replay-exposure experiments resolve a central evaluation confound. Coverage remains three models, two primary environments, and six trials.
- Lark research document: [Evaluating Agent Reflection Under Finite Trial Budgets](https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/ZlTOdrlZioptROxeGjqcaenDnTg).

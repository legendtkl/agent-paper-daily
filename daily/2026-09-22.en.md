### [Building Coding-Agent RL Environments Directly from Source](../papers/2026/09/2609.22068.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.22068 | 95/100 | primary [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at); secondary [`SE`](../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev), [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy).
- CodeMidas generates tasks, adapted repositories, and executable verifiers from source, retaining 5,545 tasks after six-container consistency, leakage attacks, and solution review. MiMo-V2.5 improves on five external benchmarks, including DeepSWE 10.0% to 21.7% and ProgramBench Almost Solved 4.5% to 21.5%; tasks, construction code, weights, and per-task outputs are not yet public.
- Selection reason: source-derived environments expand the feasible RL-data boundary; transfer across five software-task types and a filtered-5k versus uncleaned-8k ablation are substantive. A single model, no multi-seed uncertainty, and unavailable artifacts reduce reproducibility.

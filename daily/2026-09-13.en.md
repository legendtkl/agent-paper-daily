## Selected paper

### [Contextual-bandit-guided Agent Skill evolution](../papers/2026/09/2609.11682.en.md)

- arXiv:2609.11682 | 86/100 | Primary [`LT`](../docs/categories.en.md#lt); secondary [`AF`](../docs/categories.en.md#af), [`TU`](../docs/categories.en.md#tu)
- COBRA-Skills uses a neural reward predictor and LinearUCB to allocate Skill-evaluation budget, then periodically regenerates, mutates, and crosses candidates using execution evidence. It has the highest average score across six benchmarks and three target models, with estimated costs 55–58% below SkillOpt.
- Selection rationale: it directly advances continual Agent Skill optimization and provides cross-model, cross-harness, ablation, and cost evidence plus a public implementation whose basic tests pass. Optimization-data budgets differ across methods, and the full study has not been independently rerun.
- Lark research document: <https://bytedance.larkoffice.com/docx/AxKQdgbl1ow3gVx1d2WcXSqmntb>

- [2611.27182](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27182) (Tripwire, 93; primary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss); secondary [`EA`](../docs/categories.en.md#ea), [`AF`](../docs/categories.en.md#af)): TRIP lets an agent write lightweight interrupts for a continuously executing robot policy. Version 2 only adds an equal-contribution note for Sunli Chen and Yun Zong; the method and evidence are unchanged. No paper-specific implementation is public, and real-robot evidence covers only two tasks and ten tabletop setups.
- [2611.27860](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27860) (SplitBench, 94; primary [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary [`SS`](../docs/categories.en.md#ss), [`SE`](../docs/categories.en.md#se)): SplitBench separates untrusted test execution from trusted grading. It converts 180 of 202 Terminal-Bench and DeepSWE tasks, agrees with native graders in 98.7% of 1,356 comparisons, and reports that none of 360 adversarial attempts passed through a SplitBench flaw. The full artifact remains unavailable, leaving the converted tasks, attack records, and Table 5 recomputation script unauditable.

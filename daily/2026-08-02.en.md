## Selected Papers

1. [Echoverse: Deep, Evolving Environments for Training Computer-Use Agents at Scale](../papers/2026/08/2607.28074.en.md) | primary category [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at); secondary categories [`CU`](../docs/categories.en.md#cu), [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev) | 88/100
   - The paper packages environments, tasks, and verifiers into resettable worlds, using failed trajectories to drive both environment repair and model training. The 9B model improves from 36.5% to 67.1% across 14 splits, while held-out RL performance across five worlds rises from 58.8% to 68.0%.
   - Four environments, 722 evaluation tasks, and a verification harness are public, but the complete training environments, corpus, training code, and checkpoint have not been released.
   - Attention: 9 Hugging Face votes; 22 GitHub stars and 3 forks. Collected at 2026-08-02 09:08 (Asia/Shanghai).

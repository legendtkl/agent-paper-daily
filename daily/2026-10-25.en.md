## Selected paper

### [Reconstructing Verifiable App Worlds from Traces](../papers/2026/10/2610.30792.en.md)

- arXiv:2610.30792 | 96/100 | Primary [`SY`](../docs/categories.en.md#sy); secondary [`CU`](../docs/categories.en.md#cu), [`AT`](../docs/categories.en.md#at), and [`EV`](../docs/categories.en.md#ev).
- Trace2World reconstructs 50 app worlds from 1,570 real traces while isolating shared semantics, executable implementation, source-replay acceptance, and task expansion behind bounded interfaces. Across 200 workflows from ten apps, all-six fidelity is 91.1% versus 59.8% for the strongest matched reimplementation; source acceptance also raises retained real-device gain from 54.7% under self-validation to 94.0%. The paper claims a resource release, but no verifiable paper-specific repository entry point was found and this run does not independently reproduce the experiments.

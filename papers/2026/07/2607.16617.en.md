# DataFlow-Harness: A Grounded Code-Agent Platform for Constructing Editable LLM Data Pipelines

English | [中文](2607.16617.md)

> Primary category: [`SE`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#se); secondary categories: [`TU`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#tu), [`SY`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#sy)

## Summary

Uses a live operator registry, typed mutations, and visual DAGs to let agents generate platform-native data pipelines.

## Experiments and Evidence

12 tasks with 10 runs per task; the authors report a 93.3% end-to-end pass rate, together with lower cost and latency.

## Limitations and Risks

The task set is small, and the gains depend on platform-specific assets and operator quality.

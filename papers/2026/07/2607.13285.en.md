# Harness Handbook: Making Evolving Agent Harnesses Readable, Navigable, and Editable

English | [中文](2607.13285.md)

> Primary category: [`AF`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#af); secondary categories: [`SE`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`SY`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#sy)

## Summary

Generates a harness documentation tree organized by behavior rather than file structure, allowing agents to navigate progressively to source-code anchors.

## Experiments and Evidence

Two open-source harnesses and 60 modification requests; reported plan win rate increases while planning-token use decreases.

## Limitations and Risks

The evaluation focuses on plan quality and does not yet directly establish final-patch correctness.

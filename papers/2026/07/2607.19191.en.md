# ABot-World-0: Infinite Interactive World Rollout on a Single Desktop GPU

English | [中文](2607.19191.md)

> Primary category: [`EA`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ea); secondary categories: [`CU`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#cu), [`SY`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#sy)

## Summary

Turns an action-conditioned world model into a low-latency closed-loop environment while addressing long-horizon drift and character consistency.

## Experiments and Evidence

The authors report 720p output at up to 16 FPS and 1.2-second first-frame latency on a single RTX 5090.

## Limitations and Risks

Visual and action metrics cannot substitute for real-robot or task-success evaluation.

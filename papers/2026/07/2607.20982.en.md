# GuardianAgentBench: Where Agents Fail and How to Guard Them

English | [中文](2607.20982.md)

> Primary category: [`SS`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ss); secondary categories: [`EV`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ev), [`TU`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#tu)

## Summary

Uses execution DAGs to distinguish omitted calls, incorrect tool selection, argument errors, and ordering errors, and adds a runtime guardrail before tool execution.

## Experiments and Evidence

580 scenarios, six business domains, and 81 tools; the authors report gains of 2.8–7.7 points from the guard.

## Limitations and Risks

The guard depends on a closed model; latency, tokens, and cost are not fully reported, and the data and code are not public.

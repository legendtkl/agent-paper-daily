# SEED: Self-Evolving On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

English | [中文](2607.14777.md)

> Primary category: [`AT`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#at); secondary categories: [`LT`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`RP`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#rp)

## Summary

Converts on-policy trajectories into hindsight skills and distills skill-induced changes in token probabilities back into the policy.

## Experiments and Evidence

Covers ALFWorld, search QA, and WebShop; component ablations support the on-policy synchronization hypothesis.

## Limitations and Risks

Natural-language skills may amplify the model's self-explanation bias; open-ended tools and cost require further testing.

# Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench

English | [中文](2607.08964.md)

> Primary category: [`EV`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary categories: [`SE`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`SY`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#sy)

## Summary

Evaluates long-duration terminal tasks with fine-grained subtasks and intermediate rewards, preventing a final-step failure from erasing all prior progress.

## Experiments and Evidence

46 tasks and 17 models; an average of 9.8M tokens and about 239 episodes per task.

## Limitations and Risks

The task and grader sets are limited, and dense grading may favor specific implementation paths.

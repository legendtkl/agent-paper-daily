# DeepSearch-World: Self-Distillation for Deep Search Agents in a Verifiable Environment

English | [中文](2607.07820.md)

> Primary category: [`RA`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ra); secondary categories: [`RP`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#rp), [`EV`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ev)

## Summary

Trains deep-search agents with a deterministic Wikipedia search environment, process states, and 11 rounds of evolutionary SFT.

## Experiments and Evidence

420K tasks; the authors report systematic gains for a 9B model across six benchmarks, including BrowseComp, GAIA, and HotpotQA.

## Limitations and Risks

An offline Wikipedia environment may overestimate structured retrieval capability; transfer to the live web remains to be verified.

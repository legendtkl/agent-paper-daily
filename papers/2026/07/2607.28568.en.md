# Frontis-MA1: Training an AI4AI Model towards Recursive Self-Improvement in Machine Learning Engineering

English | [中文](2607.28568.md)

> Primary category: [`AT`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#at); secondary categories: [`LT`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`SE`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#se)

## Summary

Unifies training-time and test-time improvement through executable MLE environments, program-evolution operators, and long-horizon search.

## Experiments and Evidence

On MLE-Bench Lite, Medal Average rises from 39.39% to 60.61%, while Evo-Max reaches 71.21%.

## Limitations and Risks

Results are sensitive to search budget and task selection, and the work remains primarily a single generation of meta-evolution.

# Function-Aware Fill-in-the-Middle as Mid-Training for Coding Agent Foundation Models

English | [中文](2607.12463.md)

> Primary category: [`SE`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#se); secondary categories: [`PT`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#pt), [`TU`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#tu)

## Summary

Constructs fill-in-the-middle objectives from function-call structure, embedding an action–observation–continuation inductive bias into code foundation models.

## Experiments and Evidence

968 Python repositories and about 2.6B tokens, with evaluation spanning SWE-Bench, Terminal-Bench, τ-bench, and BFCL.

## Limitations and Risks

Validation focuses on the Qwen family and Python data; transfer across models and languages remains uncertain.

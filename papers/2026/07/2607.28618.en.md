# AskChem: Claim-Centered Infrastructure for Chemistry Literature Synthesis

English | [中文](2607.28618.md)

> Primary category: [`RA`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ra); secondary categories: [`KM`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#km), [`EV`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ev)

## Summary

Changes the retrieval unit from papers to scientific claims linked to DOIs and source-text locations, exposing them to agents through MCP.

## Experiments and Evidence

About 147K papers and 2.4M claims; DOI resolution is 100% across 30 questions.

## Limitations and Risks

A resolvable DOI does not establish factual correctness; the question set is small and full-text extraction coverage is limited.

## Community Assessment

Verifiable attention captured during the historical retrospective: 289 Hugging Face votes, 2 comments, and 7 GitHub stars. Interaction volume indicates discussion activity only, not research quality. This migration does not add unverifiable positions from the comments.

## Research Assessment

The internal retrospective score is 81/100. This score was used to rank candidates within the month; key claims should still be checked against the paper body, tables, and code.

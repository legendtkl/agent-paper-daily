## Core contribution

Harness-of-Harness treats the coding harness itself as a persistent artifact: the agent edits tools and instructions across days, preserves state, and evaluates whether those changes improve later work.

## Selection rationale and evidence

Cross-benchmark results, budget controls, ablations, and per-task records make the central claim auditable; the paper was selected for directly testing durable improvement rather than one-shot patch generation.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

Core implementation details and full generated artifacts were not public at collection time, and several results rely on single generated variants or limited seeds.

## Community and open-source observations

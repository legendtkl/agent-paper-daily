## Core contribution

APIFlow-Bench evaluates long REST workflows with deterministic worlds, terminal-state checks, and source-sensitive delivery scoring, separating correct state changes from incorrect final reporting.

## Selection rationale and evidence

The release covers 19 models, 467 tasks, and 44,362 traces. Clean 20-subtask chains passed at 74.4% versus 92.9% for single tasks; 169 of 219 clean failures reached the correct state but failed final delivery.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

About 8% of chain trials were affected by instruction-rewrite omissions, only 11 worlds supported the 20-step claim, and task and oracle generation share a model-family bias.

## Community and open-source observations

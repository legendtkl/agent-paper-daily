## Core contribution

Repo-To-Skill converts repositories into executable, test-backed Agent Skills and evaluates whether the resulting artifacts preserve behavior rather than merely summarize documentation.

## Selection rationale and evidence

The work combines repository analysis, generated skill files, verification tasks, and cost accounting. It was selected for making a skill an auditable engineering artifact with explicit acceptance tests.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

Cross-model transfer and end-to-end cost including skill construction were not independently reproduced, and some gains may depend on the benchmark's repository and verifier design.

## Community and open-source observations

Checked 2026-09-04: 496 Hugging Face votes, 115 GitHub stars, and eight forks. Public commentary was supportive of verifiable skills but did not provide an independent full rerun.

## Core contribution

The benchmark separates functional correctness from code-review constraints, showing that a patch may pass tests while violating requested design, scope, style, or maintainability requirements.

## Selection rationale and evidence

The paper uses repository changes with explicit review constraints and compares functional and review-oriented judgments. It was selected because it exposes a production-relevant blind spot in coding-agent evaluation.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

Review constraints are harder to make exhaustive and objective than executable tests; coverage, judge reliability, and cross-repository transfer remain the main evidence boundaries.

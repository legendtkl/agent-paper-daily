## Core contribution

The study isolates how startup probes, shell access, and screenshots change coding-agent outcomes instead of comparing whole harnesses as indivisible packages.

## Selection rationale and evidence

Across 1,116 builds, six models, and eight tool configurations, a startup probe raised launch success from 86.5% to 99.5%; full shell access mainly helped concurrency, restart, and ordering stress tests.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

A single human scorer, a non-interactive screenshot tool, and one application domain limit generalization. The listed GitHub repository returned 404, although a Zenodo archive was reachable.

## Community and open-source observations

No verifiable independent technical review was found by 2026-09-02. Hugging Face had no page, Hacker News had no exact match, Reddit access was limited, and X engagement was not verified.

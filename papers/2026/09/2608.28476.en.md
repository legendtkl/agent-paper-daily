## Core contribution

ContextPilot lets an agent plan, write long-term memory, retrieve, and softly offload context, then trains those editing decisions with branch-focused exploration and snapshot-level credit assignment.

## Selection rationale and evidence

Its 8B RL model averaged 69.40 across four long-context QA benchmarks, 3.55 points above StateLM-8B-RL, while keeping BrowseComp inputs near 8K-10K tokens instead of roughly 30K. Results span several backbones and two task families.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

Training uses many branched snapshots and does not report complete compute cost. Coding and GUI agents were not evaluated, and no independent benchmark reproduction was found.

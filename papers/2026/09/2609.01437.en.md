## Core contribution

HarnessDev changes the unit of evaluation from a task answer to an executable, frozen, auditable harness, testing both creation from a weak seed and continued evolution from runtime feedback.

## Selection rationale and evidence

Creation spans six creator models, four domains, five downstream benchmarks, and 2,207 unique instances. Hidden tasks, frozen versions, runtime audits, and cross-executor comparisons expose the gap between writing a harness and improving it reliably.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

Evolution often has one trajectory per setting, only two of nine declared versions were also best on held-out tasks, and code, generated artifacts, and complete traces were not public.

## Community and open-source observations

Checked 2026-09-04: 225 Hugging Face votes and several public analyses. A concrete critique highlighted heterogeneous human references, low evolution sample counts, and weak held-out selection; Reddit was inaccessible and exact X engagement was not verified.

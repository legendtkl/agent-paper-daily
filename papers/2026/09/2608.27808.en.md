## Core contribution

CURA turns harness-visible telemetry into an online CUSUM alarm with an explicit false-positive budget, without reading model internals or adding another LLM call.

## Selection rationale and evidence

Across 361 OSWorld tasks, 64 of 71 failed runs still claimed success. At alpha=0.10, the alarm reached a 0.066 observed false-positive rate, 42.3% recall, and a median lead of 31 steps.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

The certificate controls false alarms on the calibrated success distribution; it does not certify task correctness or safety. Evidence comes from one pipeline and one benchmark, and the code was not public at collection time.

## Community and open-source observations

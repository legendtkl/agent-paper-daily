## Core contribution

MaxKernel combines planning, implementation, compilation repair, frozen tests, autotuning, profiling, and graph search to generate TPU Pallas kernels from measured hardware feedback.

## Selection rationale and evidence

Parallel and beam search reached 50/50 compilable and correct tasks with 1.58x and 1.49x geometric-mean speedups. On eight expert tasks, parallel search reached 2.32x versus 2.02x for handwritten Pallas, with five independent Auto runs.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

All LLM calls used one model, tests ran on a single TPU v6e device, some tolerances were as loose as 1e-1, and total token and TPU search cost were not reported.

## Community and open-source observations

Checked 2026-09-09: 12 Hugging Face votes and 69 stars on the broader accelerator-agents repository. An independent issue proposed reruns and questioned tolerance, single-device coverage, and contamination; it was a test plan, not a reproduction.

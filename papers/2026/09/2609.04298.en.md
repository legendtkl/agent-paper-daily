## Core contribution

The work unifies repeated environment, runner, and scoring machinery across Agent benchmarks while compressing execution artifacts so that experiments remain comparable and cheaper to store and rerun.

## Selection rationale and evidence

It was selected for treating evaluation infrastructure as a measurable systems object, with cross-benchmark integration, reproducible execution boundaries, and explicit storage or runtime savings.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

Unified wrappers can hide benchmark-specific assumptions, and reported efficiency does not by itself prove semantic equivalence across environments. Independent cross-runner reproduction was not found.

## Community and open-source observations

Checked 2026-09-08: verifiable community discussion was limited; specified Reddit endpoints were inaccessible and X engagement was not verified. Attention metrics were treated as distribution evidence only.

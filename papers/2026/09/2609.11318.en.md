## Conclusion summary

- Mr.LHDR scores both final answers and dependency-consistent stated conclusions.
- Its 102 real-world questions across eight categories average 12.1 necessary conclusions and dependency depth 10.4; every item has non-text evidence that changes the reasoning state.
- GPT-5.5 has the highest reported OA point estimate (43.1%), with 34.3% SA and 70.3% DACS. Most adjacent systems are not statistically separable.
- Code, data, run, judge, and score workflows are available, but the small set, LLM judge, and lack of direct grounding verification remain material limits.

## Method and evidence

Each item provides a short verifiable answer, a checklist of necessary conclusions, and a dependency graph. OA measures final-answer accuracy; SA requires both the answer and every checklist step; CS counts correct steps; DACS counts a step only when it and all transitive prerequisites are correct. Thus DACS is at most CS. The evaluation spans tool-augmented VLMs, tool-free VLMs, dedicated deep-research products, and DeerFlow agents.

In Table 4, GPT-5.5 reports 43.1% OA, 34.3% SA, 73.6% CS, and 70.3% DACS. o3 Deep Research reports 32.4%, 19.6%, 56.1%, and 49.8%; DeerFlow with Qwen3-VL-235B reports 15.7%, 9.8%, 41.8%, and 38.5%. The paper uses 10,000 bootstrap resamples and explicitly says most neighboring rankings are not separable.

Figure 5 freezes the 102 items, references, checklists, and judge and removes only images for Qwen3-VL-235B. Images improve DACS by 12.6 points (95% CI [6.9, 18.9]) and CS by 12.4 points (CI [6.9, 18.3]).

## Limitations, reproduction, and use

The authors instantiate only one open-source framework, acknowledge limited ranking resolution with 102 items, and score submitted responses rather than latent reasoning or tool traces. DACS checks stated dependency consistency, not source reliability, accurate citation, or leak-free retrieval. The repository supplies download, decryption, run, judge, and scoring commands; model execution needs compatible model, search, image, and judge services. It is useful for regression testing research agents and diagnosing multimodal evidence chains, not for direct production model selection from a single 102-item score.

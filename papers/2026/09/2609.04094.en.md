## Core contribution

The method assigns credit to intermediate long-horizon decisions without requiring a hand-built step verifier, using alternative continuations and downstream outcomes to estimate local value.

## Selection rationale and evidence

The study reports gains across long-horizon settings and includes comparisons intended to separate the credit mechanism from extra sampling. It was selected because it targets a central training bottleneck when only terminal outcomes are available.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

Counterfactual sampling is expensive, the learned credit can inherit policy and judge bias, and public artifacts did not support an independent end-to-end reproduction at collection time.

## Community and open-source observations

Early Hugging Face and Hacker News checks showed limited verifiable discussion. The specified Reddit endpoints were inaccessible, X engagement was not verified, and no independent reproduction was found.

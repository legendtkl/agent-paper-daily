## Core contribution

Terminal-Universe replays file-state changes from terminal-agent traces, lets an agent complete the environment, and generates verifiable single-workspace, cross-workspace, and multi-turn tasks.

## Selection rationale and evidence

Qwen3.5-27B improved from 46.2% to 58.1% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, while EvoCode-Bench v2 MT@4 rose from 6.3 to 20.1. The design ties environment construction to executable validation.

## Evidence and reproducibility limits

Code, reconstructed environments, and trained models were not public at collection time, so trace fidelity, contamination, and generation cost could not be independently checked.

## Conclusion summary

- SimuGuard shows that some failures in robot benchmarks are caused by contact simulation, not the agent policy: an object already placed in a container can be ejected in a single 4 ms physics step.
- Across five RoboTwin container tasks, 40 of LingBot-VA's 44 failures and 6 of HyVLA's 10 failures contain an artifact. The benchmark's scripted demonstrations fail in the same way.
- The cause is a fixed triangle-area threshold in PhysX 5.3.1 distance computation. Replacing it with the PhysX 5.4.0 threshold reduces artifact episodes from 12/100 to zero and raises LingBot-VA success from 89% to 98%.
- Simulated evaluation should preserve physics-step evidence, replay anomalies, and report an attribution range when the engine cannot be repaired rather than only one success rate.

## Research question and background

LIBERO, RoboCasa, and RoboTwin treat the simulator as a trusted environment. Their success checks record whether the task ended correctly, not whether the policy or physics engine caused a failure. If the engine ejects an object after a correct placement, the run still lowers the agent score and can corrupt rankings or training feedback.

The paper asks whether contact artifacts occur across benchmarks, whether they can be traced to a concrete solver mechanism, and whether a one-variable engine repair changes closed-loop results reproducibly.

## Method

SimuGuard is a side-channel auditor attached to the benchmark's original physics `step`. It does not modify the policy or checker. Each substep records actuation, body state, mass, contact pairs, impulses, penetration depth, and a bound on speed that robot contact could have imparted. Detectors flag contact ejection, deep penetration, impulse spikes, non-finite states, and speeds beyond the actuation bound.

Replay rebuilds the same seeded scene, applies every recorded action from the first step, and checks state fidelity. Intervention keeps actions and success checks fixed while changing one simulation parameter. Five robotics researchers also blindly judge detected clips.

Root-cause analysis finds that centimeter-scale valid triangles fall below a fixed threshold in PhysX GJK distance computation. Early triangle rejection reports about two centimeters of false penetration, which the contact solver removes in one step and ejects the object at several meters per second.

## Experiments and evidence

- The audit covers 2,060 LingBot-VA and 220 HyVLA RoboTwin episodes, plus 40 RoboDojo, 400 LIBERO, and 300 RoboCasa episodes.
- LingBot-VA reports 83.1% on five container tasks. Treating every flagged episode as failure or success yields 75.8–98.5%. This is an attribution range, not a confidence interval.
- The study traces 20 false and four real contacts. The PhysX 5.4.0 threshold removes all 20 false contacts while retaining all four real contacts.
- In five tasks with 20 seeds each, LingBot-VA rises from 89/100 to 98/100 and artifact episodes fall from 12 to zero. HyVLA rises from 90/100 to 94/100 and artifacts fall from ten to one.
- Exact replay covers 96/96 RoboTwin failures, 400/400 LIBERO episodes, 13/13 RoboDojo episodes, and 25 RoboTwin episodes on another machine. Monitored and unmonitored trajectories are bit-identical for recorded bodies.
- Five robotics researchers judge 37 of 40 detected clips physically implausible, precision 0.925, and reject all 20 hard negatives.

## Limitations and risks

The repaired-engine closed loop covers two policies, five tasks, and 20 seeds per task. On 83 shared LingBot-VA seeds, the success difference alone is not significant (`p = 0.11`); the clearest effect is the artifact count.

Only one PhysX 5.3.1 constant is changed, and RoboTwin is not rerun on a complete PhysX 5.4.0 build. The RoboDojo root cause is unknown, LIBERO and RoboCasa audits are smaller, and human precision is measured on one task. Detector outputs remain review candidates, not automatic truth.

The public repository is `0.1.0.dev0` and documents only one validated RoboTwin/SAPIEN adapter. It has no license. The paper promises recordings with the code, but the current single-commit repository does not include complete paper runs.

## Reproduction and application

This review pins commit `65c0ef5622feebcbd55796aff0c2c8dc8b0014bc`. Under Python 3.13, `pytest -q tests` passes 40 tests and skips one because MuJoCo is not installed. Running pytest over the entire repository also collects a mechanism-development script containing an author-machine absolute path, so validation is limited to the formal `tests/` directory.

These checks cover core state types, detectors, recording, replay, RoboTwin helpers, and no-MuJoCo degradation. They do not run SAPIEN, PhysX, GPUs, or paper policies and therefore do not independently reproduce 2,060 RoboTwin episodes or the closed-loop score changes.

Operational use should preserve seeds, per-step actions, contacts, and checker outcomes, send anomalies to human review, and reassign failure from policy to simulator only after a repair or one-variable replay changes the outcome.

## Related work

- [RoboTwin 2.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18088) provides bimanual simulated evaluation; SimuGuard finds that PhysX artifacts enter its success rate.
- [LIBERO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03310) evaluates lifelong robot learning; the paper observes fewer and slower MuJoCo anomalies there.
- [RoboCasa](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02523) provides kitchen simulation; 300 episodes are audited without locating the cause.
- [LIBERO-PRO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03827) perturbs tasks to test policy robustness; SimuGuard asks whether the simulator itself creates failure.
- [Deep RL at the Edge of the Statistical Precipice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264) motivates multi-seed statistical reporting; this work adds an environment-attribution range distinct from confidence intervals.

## Research judgment

- Reading priority: high. It changes how embodied-agent benchmark failures should be attributed.
- Confidence: medium-high. Root cause, one-variable repair, exact replay, and blinded ratings form a strong chain; cross-engine transfer and power remain limited.

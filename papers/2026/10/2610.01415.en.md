## Conclusion summary

- PoS replaces compressed interaction history as the decision context with an explicit belief containing current world state, task objective, unresolved epistemic gaps, and unfinished achievement gaps.
- It validates evidence for belief updates, monitors whether actions make progress, diagnoses Static, Cycle, or Drift trapping, and composes recovery constraints for the specific trap and gap type.
- PoS leads every evaluated method in all 12 benchmark–backbone combinations across ALFWorld, LOCA-Bench, RCA-100, ClinDiag and three backbones.
- Consistency and trapping ablations degrade across all four benchmarks, but RCA-100 with Qwen3.7-Plus consumes 5.06× the tokens of Raw Trajectory.
- Code, four benchmark adapters, data preparation, and ablation profiles are public. Four external baselines and historical outputs are absent, and API model snapshots are not pinned.

## Research question and background

Long context, summarization, and external memory preserve history but do not guarantee a coherent answer to what is true now, what remains unknown, and what still needs to be done. Raw history is redundant; compression may discard decisive facts; neither directly identifies an agent that keeps acting without approaching its goal.

PoS reframes context management as belief maintenance and progress monitoring. It tests whether explicit, validated, gap-conditioned beliefs outperform raw trajectories and existing context-management methods across execution and diagnostic tasks.

## Method

Belief Modeling maintains `B_t=(W_t,G,Δ_t^E,Δ_t^A)`: world state, goal, epistemic gaps, and achievement gaps. The Entity–State–Relation world model records provenance, confidence, and supporting evidence while retaining only decision-relevant information.

Belief-Guided Interaction selects an Active Gap before each action. The Belief Sentinel validates consistency and evidential support and records task-relevant progress. Trapping-Aware Recovery aggregates gap persistence, stagnation, and belief recurrence to diagnose Static, Cycle, or Drift dynamics, then composes constraints for the trap and blocked gap.

PoS runs in a shared ReAct loop without weight updates. Baselines include Raw Trajectory, ACON, PACE, HiAgent, and LongHorizon-Harness; ablations remove consistency validation or trapping diagnosis and recovery.

## Experiments and evidence

The evaluation covers 134 ALFWorld valid-unseen tasks, 525 LOCA-Bench cases, all 103 RCA-100 cases, and a fixed 604-case ClinDiag subset. Metrics are task success, task success, joint fault-type/root-cause accuracy, and diagnosis accuracy.

PoS scores 88.81/56.38/38.83/45.03 with Qwen3.7-Plus, 94.03/74.29/51.46/54.80 with Kimi-K3, and 97.01/70.86/49.51/52.15 with GLM-5.3. Each value is the best overall result within its benchmark–backbone group. Maximum relative gains over the strongest same-backbone baseline are 22.68%, 7.53%, 37.89%, and 11.31%.

Removing consistency costs up to 14.93 points on ALFWorld and 11.81 on LOCA-Bench. Removing trapping diagnosis costs 4.85–6.79 points on RCA-100 and 2.65–3.97 on ClinDiag. A generic recovery prompt is weaker than factorized recovery on all four benchmarks.

On RCA-100/Qwen3.7-Plus, Raw Trajectory reaches 24.27% joint accuracy with 355.70K tokens per case. PoS reaches 38.83%; Task Agent usage falls to 281.20K, but Belief and Sentinel add 696.69K and 822.24K, totaling 1,800.13K or 5.06×.

LOCA Environment Operations remains only 36.00%–41.14% even with PoS. Some ClinDiag failures occur after correct observations because the backbone lacks medical knowledge. Better belief state does not replace execution skills or domain knowledge.

## Limitations and risks

The authors acknowledge that repeated natural-language belief generation adds token/compute cost, redundancy, omission, and semantic distortion. PoS does not systematically retrieve external domain knowledge, so an internally consistent belief can still rest on incorrect knowledge or causal interpretation.

Our assessment is that performance and added inference budget are coupled because the same backbone fills policy, belief, and sentinel roles. The paper gives token counts but not a complete dollar-cost, latency, or throughput ledger. Fixed thresholds, windows, and audit cadence have not been shown to transfer without tuning.

The repository contains no historical outputs and omits the ACON, PACE, HiAgent, and LongHorizon-Harness implementations. API models have no fixed snapshot. The release correctly separates offline control-flow checks from benchmark reproduction.

## Related work

- [LongHorizon-Harness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01964) maintains independently auditable task state; PoS targets uncertain inference and gaps that cannot be immediately verified.
- [AgentFold](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24699) proactively folds history; PoS maintains an explicit estimate of the current world instead of centering history compression.
- [StateAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02810) adds chain-of-states; PoS adds evidence validation, gap modeling, health monitoring, and targeted recovery.
- [Graph of States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21250) constrains abductive reasoning with causal graphs and state machines; PoS generalizes explicit belief to long-horizon execution and diagnosis.

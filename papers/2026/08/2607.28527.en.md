# MANTA

English | [中文](2607.28527.md)

> Primary category: [`MA`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ma); secondary categories: [`LT`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#lt), [`AF`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#af)

## Summary

- MANTA monitors collaboration traces during task execution and applies constrained rewiring, reordering, or addition of verification roles.
- The authors report an average success rate of 74.0±1.8 across five benchmarks, 5.8 percentage points above the strongest baseline.
- Structural repairs are applied in 151 of 450 runs, showing that dynamic topology adjustment is exercised in practice rather than remaining conceptual.
- A cleaner process does not guarantee a correct outcome; no public code repository was found during the historical collection.

## Research Question and Background

Multi-agent systems usually fix their roles and communication structure before execution, but error types and verification needs can change over a trajectory. A fixed topology may create redundant communication or omit a verification role at a critical step.

## Core Method

The system continuously observes collaboration traces and adjusts connections, role order, and verification nodes within a constrained action space, allowing the communication structure to respond to failure signals during execution.

## Experiments and Evidence

The primary evidence includes average success across five benchmarks, a 5.8-percentage-point gain over the strongest baseline, and 151 actual repairs in 450 runs. Per-benchmark results, variance, and ablations are still needed to assess the stability of the gains.

## Limitations and Risks

Topology repair depends on reliable process signals; a process that appears sound can still produce an incorrect result. Dynamic adjustment also increases system complexity and debugging cost.

## Reproducibility and Application

The approach may apply to long tool-use workflows, multi-role verification, and agent systems requiring runtime fault tolerance. Reproduction is costly without a public implementation.

## Community Assessment

No verifiable Hacker News, Reddit, X, or GitHub interaction metrics were found as of the historical collection time.

## Research Assessment

Treating communication topology as an object of runtime improvement is valuable. Confidence in the conclusions depends on code release, broader task distributions, and complete reporting of additional cost.

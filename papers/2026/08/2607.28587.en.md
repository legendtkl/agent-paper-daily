# PAIChecker

English | [中文](2607.28587.md)

> Primary category: [`EV`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ev); secondary categories: [`SE`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#se), [`MA`](../../../docs/categories.en.md#ma)

## Summary

- The paper audits whether problem descriptions in SWE-bench Verified actually align with their corresponding pull requests, reporting 68 misaligned examples among 500 instances, or 13.6%.
- The method combines three specialized agents, evidence synthesis, and code-level verification to reduce false positives from a single judge.
- The authors report binary classification accuracy as high as 92.12% on SWE-Gym and 91.67% on SWE-bench Multilingual.
- Cost is about USD 0.08–0.23 per instance, but the set of examples manually confirmed in real repositories remains limited.

## Research Question and Background

Coding-agent benchmarks commonly combine issues, pull requests, and test oracles into training or evaluation instances. If an issue and pull request address different problems, a model can be rewarded or penalized incorrectly because of data misalignment.

## Core Method

PAIChecker divides the audit among specialized roles that extract issue evidence and code-change evidence, then synthesizes and verifies them at the code level to determine alignment.

## Experiments and Evidence

The central evidence is the 68/500 misalignment rate in SWE-bench Verified, cross-dataset binary classification results, and ablations of the three-stage pipeline. The historical import did not preserve full table screenshots, so key figures should be checked against the paper.

## Limitations and Risks

The manually confirmed real-repository sample is small, and the automated auditor may inherit the code-understanding biases of its component models. Conference or acceptance information is not treated as evidence of community attention.

## Reproducibility and Application

The method can serve as a preflight check for coding-agent training-set construction, benchmark revision, and test-oracle auditing.

## Community Assessment

No verifiable Hacker News, Reddit, X, or GitHub interaction metrics were found as of the historical collection time.

## Research Assessment

The paper addresses benchmark construction quality rather than model scores, an important and easily overlooked problem. Follow-up should focus on independent human verification of the audit results and tool availability.

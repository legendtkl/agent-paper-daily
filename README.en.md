## Selection policy

- The normal daily volume is 0–2 papers. A rolling seven-day window usually contains no more than 7 papers and may reach 10 only when several high-confidence papers appear together.
- The monthly target is 20–35 papers. This is a capacity range, not a quota; a month may contain fewer than 20 papers when the evidence is insufficient.
- A score of 70 is only the candidate threshold. Independent research normally requires a score of at least 78.
- Facts, author claims, community opinions, and research judgments are stated separately. Unverified evidence is labeled explicitly.
- Screenshots are limited quotations from the original paper. Each image must include its figure or table number, page, source, interpretation, and limitations.

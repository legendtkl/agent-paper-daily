# Monthly reports

English | [中文](README.md)

On the first day of each month, the previous complete calendar month is summarized. The target is 20–35 papers, but the range is not a quota. Normal archives only deduplicate the daily selections; explicitly requested historical backfills are labeled separately.

Each report includes a contribution and selection rationale for every paper, grouped directions, at least three trend themes, community and open-source observations, evidence limits, and follow-up questions.

See [2026 Agent paper trends, January–July](../docs/2026-trends.en.md) for cross-month analysis.
